WONDERLAND GAME — INSTRUCTIONS

In this game, Alice has a goal - get to Wonderland.
This game world is a journey to Wonderland. All locations are path in/into Wonderland

Allowed Commands

Enter commands using words. Commands are not case-sensitive.

Some commands and meaning

You could commands such as:
go east |	east	|	e	|	forward		|	front	
Move south |	south	|	s	|	downward	|	down
Run west |	west	|	w	|	backward	|	back

eat / drink 		- you consume an item	
carry/ take/ collect 	- you take an item
drop			- you drop an item
escape			- you use you precious stone by entering 'escape' into Play room
Follow			- follow a character
Look			- this command shows you details of where you're at
cook			- you cook something
Talk/ respond		- you speak to a character
Riddle/deal/card	- you ask for a riddle in specific locations
Joke			- you ask for a joke in a specific location
Size			- check your current size
resize			- you go back to normal size (costs you 2 gems)
Quit/ exit		- you quit the game (notice that in this game, exit does not mean exit a location, it will quit the game)
Cry			- you will cry
Help/h			- you get a list of commands

Use commands such as take or take key, carry, and drop to interact with items.
Use inventory, invent, or I to check your inventory.
Note: Enter responses using words, not numerical digits. If the game asks for a number, spell it out in words. For example, enter three instead of 3.

I will leave you to discover the rest as you discover the world of the game!

The game world contains some items such as:
Gem: you can use two gems to resize
Precious stone: you can use one precious stone to escape whims of the Queen of Hearts, King of Hearts, or the Animal game into the play room where you can go practice some riddles and listen to some dad jokes. You'd still have to go through the Animal game one way or other though 😊.
Cake: you can eat cake
Cup of tea: you can drink cup of tea

Some items may be required to complete tasks or progress through the game.

I will leave you to discover the rest as you discover the world of the game!

You may encounter other characters on your adventure to Wonderland like the white rabbit, the Caterpillar, and so on.
Use appropriate commands, such as speak, talk, follow, eat, or escape, depending on the situation.
Pay attention to the characters and the available choices before acting.

Scoring
Your score is based on the number of locations you visit.
There is no limit to the points you can earn. Keep exploring Wonderland to increase your score.

Size
Your size matters in certain locations.
Some doors can only be accessed when you are the appropriate size.
Use resize when necessary to change your size and access places you could not enter before.

Pausing the Game
Type pause to pause the game
Type play to resume the game

Help and Exit
Type help or H for assistance.
Type exit, quit, or Q to leave the game.

Explore, interact, and enjoy your adventure in Wonderland!
